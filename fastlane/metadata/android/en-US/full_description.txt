Winklo is a collection of quick solo mini-games you can finish in a spare minute.

ZIP
Start at 1. Fill every cell. Finish on the last number. Move up, down, left, or right—not diagonally. A new puzzle each day.

PATH WORDS
Trace every word across the grid. Lift your finger anytime and keep going from the last cell. A new puzzle each day.

SUDOKU
Fill every cell with 1–6. No repeats in any row, column, or region. A new puzzle each day.

Keep a streak, beat your best time, and come back tomorrow for a new puzzle.
