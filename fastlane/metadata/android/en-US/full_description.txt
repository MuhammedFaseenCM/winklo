Winklo is a daily puzzle app. Every day brings a new Zip, a new Path Words board, and a new Sudoku. Clear them at your own pace, keep a streak, and post your time on a live leaderboard. No ads.

Zip
Start at 1. Draw one path through every cell and finish on the last number. Move up, down, left, or right, not diagonally. A new puzzle each day.

Path Words
Trace every hidden word across the letter grid. Lift your finger anytime and keep going from the last cell. A new word puzzle each day.

Sudoku
Fill a 6x6 board with 1-6. No number repeats in a row, column, or box. A new puzzle each day.

Play at your pace
You get three hints per game. Leave whenever you want: the clock pauses and your run is saved. Beat your best time. One streak freeze can cover a missed day.

Leaderboard
The daily leaderboard ranks players by time. A clean run can earn Hint-free or Flawless. Sign in with Google to play. Your streak and progress sync when you switch phones.
