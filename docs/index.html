How it plays

Open. Clear. Climb.

Three short steps. No ads, no clutter — just today’s puzzles and your best time.

1

Open today’s set

Sign in with Google and pick Zip, Path Words, or Sudoku. Each day brings a fresh board for every game, the same for every player.

2

Clear at your pace

Solo runs only. Three hints a day, a reset whenever you want a fresh start, and a clean dark board — finish when you’re ready, not on a timer you didn’t ask for.

3

Climb the board

Post your time to the live daily leaderboard, race friends in a private league, and keep your streak going. Progress syncs when you switch phones.

Daily habit

Built for coming back tomorrow

Winklo is a daily puzzle app — not an endless feed. Show up, clear the set, leave with a streak.

  • Streaks with a safety net One streak per game. A freeze covers a missed day, and every seventh day in a row earns one back, up to two.
  • Leagues with friends Start a private league for up to 30 people, share the invite link, and race on your own daily and weekly boards.
  • Badges and stats Sixteen badges, a calendar of cleared days, your best and average times, and a 30-day personal-best trend.
  • Archive and practice Missed a day? Replay past dailies from the archive, or warm up on endless practice boards that never touch your streak.
  • Reminders and a home-screen widget A nudge when the puzzles are ready and another when a streak is at risk, at times you pick. The widget shows what’s left today.

The set

Three games. One daily board each.

Short solo clears you can finish in a coffee break — or linger on when you want the perfect path.

Zip

Fill every cell from 1 to the finish. Walls block the path — visit the numbers in order and light up the whole grid.

Path Words

Trace each hidden word through a sparse letter grid. Find where the words run, draw clean routes, and clear the daily list.

Sudoku

Fill a 6×6 grid so every row, column, and 2×3 box holds 1–6. A compact daily board — logic first, and hints that explain the next step instead of filling it in.
