Daily habit

Built for coming back tomorrow

Winklo is a daily puzzle app — not an endless feed. Show up, clear the set, leave with a streak.

  • Fresh puzzles every day New Zip, Path Words, and Sudoku boards reset with the calendar. Miss a day and start again — or protect a streak when a freeze is available.
  • Live daily leaderboard See who’s fastest today. Your display name and avatar show with your best clean run — not a wall of ads.
  • Streaks that stick Clear today’s puzzles to keep the fire lit. Progress syncs to your account so a reinstall doesn’t wipe your run.
  • Cloud profile, private progress Leaderboard times are public in-app. Your deeper progress stays with your signed-in account.

A quiet daily ritual — not another endless scroll.

The set

Three games. One daily board each.

Short solo clears you can finish in a coffee break — or linger on when you want the perfect path.

Zip

Fill every cell from 1 to the finish. Walls block the path — visit the numbers in order and light up the whole grid.

Path Words

Trace every word across the grid. Find the shared letters, draw clean routes, and clear the daily word list.

Sudoku

Fill every cell with 1–6. A compact daily board — logic first, no endless variants.
