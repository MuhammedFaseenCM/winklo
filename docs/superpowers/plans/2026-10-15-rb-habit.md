# Area E: habit, reminders, widget and settings — implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Build section E of `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-roadmap-batch-design.md`: earned streak freezes, smarter reminders with settings and two Android channels, the weekly recap (notification and Home card), an Android home-screen widget, and a Settings screen at `/profile/settings`.

**Architecture:** All rules are pure Dart in `lib/domain/` (freeze earning and spending in `StreakCalculator`, reminder and recap planning in `lib/domain/habit/`). One use case, `RefreshHabitSurfaces`, reads the decayed streaks, today's clears and the reminder settings, then hands typed reminders to `NotificationRepository` and a snapshot to `HomeWidgetRepository`. An app-level `HabitRefreshLifecycle` runs it on start, resume, local progress changes, sync restores, midnight and settings changes. UI lives in new files (`features/settings/`, `features/home/view/widgets/home_habit_cards.dart`, `home_freeze_badge.dart`); hotspot files get one- or two-line edits.

**Tech stack:** Flutter 3.44, flutter_bloc (Cubit), go_router 18, flutter_local_notifications 22, firebase_messaging, shared_preferences, `home_widget` 0.10 (new), Kotlin `AppWidgetProvider` + RemoteViews, bloc_test + mocktail.

## Decisions recorded up front

- "Ends at midnight" follows the spec: a streak a freeze could carry still counts as at risk tonight.
- Reminders are one-offs with live text (tonight and tomorrow), replacing the generic daily repeat; tomorrow's text assumes the app isn't opened, and any open or clear replans.
- A weekly recap with no clears is not sent.
- The announcements toggle covers the `announcements` topic only; `app_updates` stays on.
- Sound and vibration in Settings go through `SfxService` / `HapticsService` (they cache the flag), which wrap the existing repositories.
